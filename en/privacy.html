YOUR DATA

Privacy policy

Updated 10 September 2026 · Compagnon for Android, version 1

Compagnon is an independent application published by Fedi Ghalloussi. For questions or requests about your data, email fedi.ghalloussi@gmail.com.

Your texts and your device

Your texts, titles, versions, conflicts, project connections and preferences are stored in Compagnon’s private storage on your device. The app does not use a Compagnon server to host manuscripts. The publisher does not add advertising, advertising tracking, audience analytics or automatic crash reporting. The exchanges required by Dropbox and Google Play are described below.

Compagnon does not access your contacts, microphone, camera or location. Imports and exports use the files and locations you select through Android. Automatic Android backup is disabled for the app; you can export your library yourself.

Dropbox connection and transfers

Connecting Dropbox is optional when using the local sample or reading an imported library. It is required to exchange projects with your computer. Authorisation takes place on Dropbox’s website: Compagnon never receives your Dropbox password.

With your permission, Compagnon accesses the Dropbox app folder “Compagnon Manuscrits”. It reads the names, paths, identifiers, revisions and contents of connected projects. It downloads texts and recovery copies, then sends your edited RTF texts to the same Dropbox account. The app keeps the account identifier locally to prevent transfers to a different account.

Dropbox authorisation tokens are encrypted on the device using an Android Keystore key. Requests travel directly between your device and Dropbox over HTTPS. Dropbox processes the contents and technical information of these exchanges under its privacy policy.

“Disconnect Dropbox” stops scheduled transfers and deletes the tokens kept by Compagnon. It keeps texts and project connections on the phone and does not delete Dropbox files. You can also revoke Compagnon’s permission in Dropbox’s connected-app settings.

Trial and Google Play purchase

Trial start and end dates and the information needed to calculate them are saved locally. No Compagnon account is created.

Google Play handles purchases, payments, restoration and refunds. Compagnon does not receive your payment card number. It receives a signed receipt, transaction and product identifiers, a purchase token and purchase status. These are used to verify your licence, acknowledge the purchase with Google and retain your access offline.

The Google Play Billing library communicates with Google services to perform and secure these operations. Transaction data and necessary technical data are processed under the Google Privacy Policy. Compagnon does not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Google Play Billing technical information

The billing library also sends operational and diagnostic events to Google: calls to billing functions, results and errors, Compagnon and library versions, Android version, device model and technical characteristics, language, system region and network type. Random session and operation identifiers help correlate these events.

These exchanges support the operation and reliability analysis of the billing service. They can occur as soon as it is initialised, even without a purchase. Compagnon does not offer a setting to disable them. These logs do not contain your manuscripts. Google applies its own retention periods and data management tools.

Google Play age and parental permissions

For installations managed by Google Play, Compagnon uses the Play Age Signals interface to check age-related access and parental permissions. Google Play may ask you to verify your age, set up supervision or choose whether to share an age range. Compagnon never asks for an identity document or date of birth.

The app processes the returned age range, its verification source and the approval status of significant changes temporarily on the device, solely to apply age-related safeguards and legal requirements. It does not retain or log those signals, approval dates or installation identifiers, and does not send them to the publisher, Dropbox, advertising services or analytics services.

Compagnon stores only the resulting access-permission flag and the app policy version in private local preferences. These allow previously checked access to continue offline. A later verification requirement or pending or declined parental approval removes that permission. Clearing app data or uninstalling Compagnon removes these preferences. A new installation without a previous permission requires an online check before normal use.

When access is paused, new writing, purchases and Dropbox transfers are blocked. Saved manuscripts are retained and a library recovery export remains available. A network failure cannot lift a previously known restriction. Offline access cannot reflect a new remote restriction until reconnection. Google Play and Family Link manage their own verification, purchase-consent and app-revocation processes under the Google Privacy Policy.

Support and public website

If you contact support, Fedi Ghalloussi receives your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. They are used to respond to your request. Avoid sensitive data and entire manuscripts; use an anonymised example where possible.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Opening a page sends GitHub the information needed to deliver and secure it, including your IP address and technical browser information. No forms, advertising or audience analytics have been added to the site. The host’s processing is described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Purposes and retention

Data required for writing, syncing and purchases is processed to provide the features you request. Dropbox connection relies on the permission you grant that service. Support messages are processed to answer your request and follow up on reported difficulties.

Texts and history remain on your device until you clear Compagnon’s data or uninstall it. Trial data follows the same retention period. The licence receipt is kept locally for offline access; a successful Google Play inventory check can remove it if the purchase is no longer valid. Exported files and copies in Dropbox remain under your control and are not deleted when the app is uninstalled.

Support messages are kept while your request is handled, then for up to 12 months after the last exchange, unless a legal obligation or dispute requires longer retention. Google, Dropbox and hosting providers apply the retention periods in their own policies.

Your choices and rights

You can export texts and libraries, disconnect Dropbox, revoke access from Dropbox and clear local data in Android settings. Back up any texts you want to keep before deleting data. Delete exported files and Dropbox files separately if you also want to remove those copies.

You can request access to, correction or deletion of your support data, portability where applicable, or exercise your rights to object or restrict processing by emailing fedi.ghalloussi@gmail.com. The publisher cannot access your local manuscripts or Dropbox account and cannot erase them remotely. For data held by Google and Dropbox, also use their account management tools.

These services may process data outside your country under the safeguards described in their policies. If you believe your rights have not been respected, you can contact your data protection authority, including the CNIL in France.
