HELP AND SUPPORT

Getting started with Compagnon.

A copy of your project, an RTF folder and the same Dropbox account on your devices.

Set up Scrivener on Windows

  1. In Dropbox, open Apps/Compagnon Manuscrits (or Applications/Compagnon Manuscrits). Create a folder for a copy of your project. The app folder keeps this name in both languages.
  2. Place the entire .scriv project there, with an empty folder named RTF beside it. Make both folders available offline on your computer.
  3. Open this copy in Scrivener 3 for Windows. Choose File → Sync → with External Folder (Shift+F7), then select the RTF folder.
  4. Enable Draft folder syncing, snapshots before updates and automatic checking of the external folder when opening and closing the project. Choose Rich Text (RTF). Leave the restriction to a collection disabled.
  5. Click Sync, close the project and wait for Dropbox to finish uploading.
  6. On Android, open Compagnon, connect Dropbox and choose Connect a project. Select the .scriv project, then the RTF folder above Draft. Check the titles and preview before confirming.

The reference workflow uses Scrivener 3.1.6.0 for Windows. If the external folder moves or its Dropbox location is not recognised, it may stay protected: reconfigure it in Scrivener before connecting it again.

Move between devices

Close your project in Scrivener before writing on Android. Compagnon saves your changes locally, even without a connection. “Changes ready to send” means the text is still on your device.

“Sent to Dropbox” confirms the upload, but does not yet mean Scrivener has imported it. On your computer, wait for Dropbox, open the project and accept the external sync. Check your texts before your next session.

Protected text or differing versions

Some documents containing images, tables, comments, annotations or unsupported formatting stay read-only. Their previews may be incomplete. You can still export the originals.

If a conflict occurs, use “Compare versions” and keep both versions before choosing. You can also export the RTF files and open them separately in Scrivener. Keep snapshots enabled on your computer.

Back up and recover

In “Backups”, regularly export your .compagnon library outside the app. It includes texts, history and conflicts. Importing it keeps your existing active documents. Your Dropbox connection and purchase are not part of this file.

Before uninstalling the app or clearing its data, export and verify your library and important RTF files. Exported files remain in the location you chose.

Trial and purchase

Your 7 calendar days start when you import the first editable project. There is no automatic charge at the end. A one-time purchase unlocks Android version 1 and all 1.x updates. The planned French launch price is €9.99. Google Play confirms the applicable local price before payment.

To restore your purchase on another device, use the same Google Play account and “Restore my purchase” under “Trial and purchase”. Buying and restoring require a connection. You can then keep writing offline while your licence remains valid.

After the trial, reading, search, exports, backups and sending drafts you have already written remain accessible.

Contact support

Email fedi.ghalloussi@gmail.com with your Compagnon version, Android version and the steps that led to the problem. Do not send passwords or an entire manuscript; a description or anonymised example is enough to start.

For questions about your data, read the privacy policy and use the same email address.

Google Play asks for a permission check

Open Play Store from the message in Compagnon and complete the steps requested by Google or ask your supervising parent to review the app in Family Link. Return to Compagnon and choose “Check again”. If Play cannot be reached, check your connection and update Play Store. Saved writing remains on the device; “Export the entire library” is available while access is paused.

After a successful check, previously authorised access can continue offline. A new installation needs an online check. A pending or declined parental approval requires a new approval, not a purchase.
