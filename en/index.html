WRITE ON ANDROID

Your manuscript,
always at hand.

Continue writing your Scrivener texts on Android, even offline, then bring your work back to your computer with Dropbox.

Room to write.

Find your texts, capture an idea and return to the sentence you left unfinished. Changes are saved automatically on your device. Choose your reading size, font and light or dark theme.

Transfers use Scrivener’s external RTF folder. Compagnon keeps versions and protects documents whose formatting cannot be edited faithfully.

Before you begin

You need Android 8 or later, Scrivener on a computer and a Dropbox account to exchange projects. Install the Dropbox desktop client on your computer; the Dropbox Android app is not required.

Start with a copy of your project and configure external RTF sync in Scrivener. Close the project on your computer before writing on Android. Images, tables, comments and other complex content may remain read-only.

Your texts stay accessible.

Your trial starts when you import your first editable project. After it ends, reading, search, exports, backups and sending drafts you have already written remain available. A purchase lets you continue writing.
